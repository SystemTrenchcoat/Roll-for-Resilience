# Hook
___
Abraham Costa, one of the reputable fishers in Nervios, needs more hands to help him with the day's work, despite usually having enough people to fill his boat.
# Story Beats
___
### 1. Learning the details
Through one way or another, a party member hears that Abraham needs extra hands on a fishing trip. When they arrive, he says they’ll head out as soon as everything is settled and he’ll bring them back before next sunrise.

### 2. Art of negotiation
The skies are clear, the waves are calm, everything around you is at peace. Although, something feels suspicious about the way Abraham is acting. Abraham tells everyone how much he’s willing to offer them for the job, but he’s willing to negotiate within reason so long as they agree. Once a price is reached, the party moves onto his boat.

### 3. Sailing to sea
Untying the rope connecting his boat to the docks, Abraham opens the sails to bring him and the party past the Eye towards deeper waters. 

### 4. More than we signed for
With everyone on board and out far enough away from shore, Abraham reveals that the reason he needed a completely new crew for the day. He wanted people with no prior connections to him to help him dispose of “extra cargo” before doing any sort of fishing, which he then brings the party below deck to show them.

### Discarding the "Extra Cargo"
Tossing the indirect language aside, Abraham is to have the party dispose of one or two things depending on your party’s comfort level. Under the decks of his ship, Abraham will have corpses belonging to a mixture of all factions, the majority of which being city guards. Alternatively, he could have crates of stolen guard equipment, including the gauntlets they use on the citizens.

Regardless of what questionable extra cargo he’s carrying, Abraham needs you to help him toss it over the side. To accomplish this, the party needs to complete a series of successes equal to two more than the party size, with each of them being at medium to hard difficulty depending on a character’s size and strength. While failures do not cause noticeable damage to their environment, it does still slow their general progress. 

Since Abraham essentially just picked everything up and loaded it right onto his ship, you can attempt to search the “extra cargo” for bits and pieces to keep for yourself if you wish. Attempts at searching are an easy check, on success roll a d6 and characters receive the corresponding reward.

### 5. Back to normal
Now that any less than legal work has been taken care of, the party needs to help Abraham actually bring home some fish. He does still need to make a living, after all.

### Fishing
Depending on party size, characters each get 2-4 chances at catching something out of the water for Abraham. Players get a minimum of one easy and one hard check during this time, and all together the party is expected to succeed at least half of these checks to satisfy Abraham.

Fishing on the return trip to land counts towards the general mechanics, and not the ones specific to this mission. As a refresher, players roll a d20 and then roll a d6 to determine their catch. A 1-10 will use the Small Catch Table, 11-20 uses the Large Catch Table, and a 20 or higher uses the Legendary Catch Table.

### 6. Return to port
Abraham decides he’s satisfied with the amount of fish the party hauls in and takes the ship back to dock, but the party is free to keep fishing until they get back. Trying to get a better read on the party, he feigns innocence and asks if they’ve heard anything about the rebellion.

### 7. Never speak of this
Abraham pulls the party aside and very sternly lets them know that if they speak a word about any “extra cargo” that he had, nobody will believe them, and there will be consequences. After that, they receive payment for their help, and part ways.

# Setting
___
The skies are clear, the waves are calm, everything around you is at peace. Seagulls and a gentle breeze could be used sparingly to help immerse the players.

## Key Locations
-  The Costa Fishing Vessel

## Hiding Spots
Apart from going under the deck, when the party is on the water there are very few places they can go to break away from each other to take a breather. 

## Motivators
The party needs to be back by next sunrise, as Abraham tends to his family along with business needs at the start of the day, and needs time to rest. If the party is inactive for too long Abraham will chastise them, before eventually escalating to threats if they repeatedly dawdle.
