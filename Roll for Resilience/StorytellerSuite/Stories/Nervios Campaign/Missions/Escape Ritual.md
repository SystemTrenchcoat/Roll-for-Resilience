# Hook
___
*As the previous mission ends, the party is silently approached from the front by a cloaked figure. The mystical Soul Moon is to shine upon Nervios tonight, weakening the barrier between life and rebirth, so it proposes the party help it perform a ritual. It requires a few additional items to prepare, but should the party desire they could be out of the city under next moonlight.*
# Story Beats
___
This mission is one of the endings to your party's adventures in Nervios, following a path that largely doesn't involve the two main factions controlling the city's future. There is no one specific mission that precedes this one.
### 1. A deal by no name
With the air falling silent and still, the party's eyes catch a cloaked figure floating towards them and effortlessly sliding past any obstacles. 
> *"Forgive the intrusion, but I, the[[Wise Drifter]], have an offer to make you all. Tonight the magnificence of the Soul Moon shines upon us. Should my predictions prove right, I could tear open a portal for you all to escape through, if you so desire*

Votiel is withholding telling them exactly why he needs them as test subjects. Unless they persuade him, he will continue saying he'll be satisfied knowing he's achieved perfection. 

If the party seems hesitant, the Drifter insists it will see them unharmed and only needs them to take care of the simpler ingredients. Summoning a sky blue Mage Hand boasting claws, the Drifter says they need to shake one of their hands to agree. If this prompts a party member to investigate, they discover nothing out of place except for the hand being cold to the touch. If someone asks why the drifter can't simply get its own ingredients, tell them it wishes to avoid unnecessary contact with the living due to its appearance.

### 2. Gathering ingredients
Now that the party has agreed to undergo the ritual from the Drifter, it tells them the ingredients they need to find: a vial of sacred water, a dozen beeswax candles, and chalk specifically from the [[Loud House Tavern|Loud House]]. While they are doing that, the Drifter says it will take care of the last ingredient, so they will meet again in the west end's [[abandoned building]] around midnight.

Whether the party chooses to stick together or separate, they will still need to gather each ingredient from its location. 

- Vial of Sacred water
	- There are two main ways that the party can receive this.
		- Buying some from the [[Church of Aequorism|Aequorist]] priests at their Sanctuary
		- With the [[Sea Scribes of Umorism#Background|Acolyte of Umorism]] background, succeed a DC 18 Religion or Survival check to purify a container of water
	- Going to the [[Sanctuary of Aequorin|Sanctuary]] , you find it largely quiet apart from a priest and their attendant softly chatting amongst themselves since your arrival does not coincide with a service. 
	- Asking for a vial of water is far from an unusual request, but your characters may still be guilty or sinners in the eyes of the church depending on their appearance. 
	- Priests may request a character face [[Church of Aequorism#Culture|Drenching]] before receiving water
	- So long as you aren't alone, your vial of water will be seen to regardless, with the priest expecting a tithe to pay for it. 
	- Paying for the vial will be a hefty 30 SCLs. If a character only has 10-29 have the priest still accept it

- Tavern Chalk
	- Although a particular ask, on entering back into [[Loud House Tavern]] you find plenty of chalk sprawled out in plain sight to take. You only need to pilfer a stick or two, and there's plenty away from the tavernkeeper, as well as some directly behind the bar. 
	- If the party has completed [[We, Sentients]], or [[The Trial]] was their previous mission, have the Loud House staff give chalk for free if asked.
	- If the party gets seen taking some of it without asking, they'll get a confused look from the tavern-keep and questioned why chalk.


- Beeswax candles
	- Overall the most straightforward ingredient to acquire, a candlemaker named Keira has a stall down in the market selling a colored variety of candles. She is cheerfully polite, dressed in a faint yellow top and white smock, with a bee charm necklace.
	- Despite her cheerful appearance, Keira overcharges the goods she personally worked so hard for and will not haggle below 15 SCL for the dozen candles.

### 3. Preparing the Ritual
Reunited and reaching the exterior threshold, you spot several of the windows are broken and blackened by dirt. Turning the handle, the door weakly cracks open, with the stairs creaking beneath you as you step inside. In the main room, hovering above the sparsely cleaned stone is Votiel
> *"Splendid, you all have done well. Fret not, I've handled my share of the work. See for yourself.*

Outshining the columns of moonlight from holes in the ceiling is the brilliance of a [[Fallen Star]] in the palm of its Mage Hand. Producing a tome from nearby, Votiel instructs the party to start drawing a circle on the floor, to which he will add the candle emplacements. 

With the circle and candles placed, a central line is drawn from each candle towards the larger circle's center, and where they intersect the star is set. While one of you goes around lighting the candles, another douses the star with the sacred water

### 4. Ritual Time
Now that all of ritual components are in place, raising hands high the Drifter chants:

>*"By the potent child of night sky, and Soul Moon sublime, I command you grant passage to these bold souls!"*

For a moment, the suspense of silence creeps in, broken by the crackle of a newly opened portal, into a magnificent cavern with stones decorated by the most brilliant of crystals. Votiel erupts into a cacophony of cackling, unable to contain his elation.
> "*Haha! This is most wondrous, friends. You've done a great feat for me, I assure you. Fare thee well, may we tread the same waves another sunrise."

Once they have stepped over, they are separated at the edges of [[The Crystal Expanse]], a bright ethereal plane for souls awaiting their next stop in the cycle of life.

### 5. Those at peace
While each get their bearings, a vibrant amber light emanates from down the caverns, with specters walking towards it and disappearing. You feel an ethereal sense of serenity coating you, down to your core, lifting any lingering anger in your mind. 

Now the thought alone of gripping your weapon shoots painful sparks up your fingers. Venturing downwards, echoes of water droplets carry on the walls, periodically rippling the ground back at you. 

As everyone continues on their way, they see beings of pure energy whose only resemblance to humanity are stubs for arms and legs. They dot the length of your path forward and back, some staring in your direction with perplexed silence. A select few, rather than having legs, possess a whirlpool in their place, along with beads of water orbiting around their torso.

Upon investigation or insight, you deduce that those with the whirlpool possess fewer impurities, in some manner. If players try to hold a conversation with one of these souls, it will be brief and very clearly convey they aren't meant to stay here.

### 6. Face to a name
Reuniting at that colorful light, a faint smell of lightning wafts through the air as each path is blocked by faceless spirits. A sonorous voice echoes against the walls, acknowledging the party for who they are. Summoning a partial avatar composed of energy, [[Aequorin]] manifests before the party, addressing each by their true name. 

Emanating a vibrant ocean blue, the avatar's torso towers high, reaching a smidge or two over six feet. Lowering his volume, Aequorin introduces himself by his true name of Malachi.

### 7. More than an Echo
The living are not allowed to linger in the [[The Crystal Expanse|Crystal Expanse]], but Malachi understands no one plans to stay, so he lowers his guard. Caring for the mortals he loves is tiresome, so he readily takes the opportunity to relax.

After a bit of chatter, Malachi transforms the spirits blocking their path into a copy of the character's player in order to hold a dialog with them. During this period, a character can offer any guidance they feel the player could benefit from hearing, ask questions, or simply reach out and hold themself. Characters can ask up to three questions, or impart three pieces of wisdom. If a player wishes for their apparition to fade prematurely, they may ask, in character, for it to do so. 

### 8. Trial of Resolute Soul
Once everyone in the party has finished interacting with their selves, Malachi draws them forward. Despite everyone being alive, they are not exempt from the same judgement as the dead. 

Everyone must endure the [[Trial of Resolute Soul]]. It's a rather simple ordeal, party members take turns placing their hands upon a stone statue of a human with crystals sprouting from it. Malachi plainly tells everyone there will be no further exceptions, whether they want to face their trial willingly or not.

### 9. Return to the Material
As the last party member removes their hand, directly across from everyone the crackle of an emerging portal is heard. Sensing he must soon return to his work, Malachi presents a choice. However, you as the DM can make this choice for them, depending on your intent with creating these characters. 

Option one is for everyone in the party to be transported into the fields north of Nervios, free to live the next chapter of their life. Option two has the characters reborn into the modern world in their player's body, hammering home "embodying the ideal self."

#### *Option One:* Raise a glass to freedom...?
 Gazing into the portal, you see a hazy field of grass as far as one could see, as Malachi ushers you through it. Gently pushing everyone, even those trying to stay, he fades back into nothingness as he waves goodbye. Crossing back over, everyone awakens on the ground staring at the pastel pink and orange of sunrise, outside Nervios' northern gate with the collapsed stones you know all too well blocking it. Gentle air on the breeze has never smelt fresher, carrying notes of lavender which overpower that signature salt from the [[Sunderwin]]. 
 
 Turning back towards the gate, everyone curiously spots a few of the granite boulders are marked by paint on this side, along with a few gifts and flower bouquets. Honest, friendly people have still been trying to come the city, despite everything, and no one noticed. Wiping away any tears and turning to the horizon, the party realizes nothing can stop them now from journeying into the unknown, or reopening a chapter in their old life. 
 
 The only question now is, who sticks together?

#### *Option Two:* See Me on the other side
Malachi floats himself closer to the portal, reciting a small indecipherable phrase while extending out his palm.  Conjured from his hand is the grey puff of a storm cloud, and with the strike of lightning it shifts the portal from a hazy projection of grass to one completely obscured by strange azure blue fog. Gliding himself clear of the party's path, Malachi stands proud as the whirlpool beneath him begins to fade.
>*It's okay to be uncertain. You've already done the hard part, you've found the love and support of another. All there is to do now is patiently follow through.* 

Malachi gestures everyone through the portal, using a gust of wind to push those hesitant to go willingly, waving goodbye as each character crosses over. For a moment, your surroundings are pitch black, then another, then slowly you see the light inch back in. You hold out your hands and see them engulfed in a shroud of light, making its way up your arms, eventually moving out of sight as it caresses your face. 

By now, the sheer luminance of your self and your companions makes it physically impossible to keep your eyes open any longer, as the soft warmth of the light soaks into your core. That feeling of uncertainty quietly dwells inside of you, yet you pay it no mind.  Once the warmth fades, you feel the beckoning to open your eyes, to carry forward that light you became.

## Hiding Spots
Apart from interacting with the Drifter and Aequorin, players are not required to talk with anyone else during the course of this mission. In keeping with this, the absolute minimum a player must do is place their hand on the Trial of Resolute Soul statue. If a player choses, they can split from everyone and go directly to the abandoned building. Spotlight this character before everyone reunites by asking how they pass the time.

## Motivators
- Sun close to setting
- Body parts start fading
- Souls disappearing instead of holding conversation
- Being forced into [[Trial of Resolute Soul|Resolute Soul]] Trial
- Other players acting as a Trial Conduit
