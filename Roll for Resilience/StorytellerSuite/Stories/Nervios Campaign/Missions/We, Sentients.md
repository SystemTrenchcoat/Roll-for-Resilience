# Hook
___
*As the [[Loud House Tavern|Loud House]] sits emptied, [[Donic]] senses the party, fresh from [[The Burrow]], making their way towards the stairs and calls them over. He introduces himself and clears any tension, just an old bot lost in cherishing the past, he says. In fact, he could use the party's help finding goodies his creator left behind.*
# Story Beats
___
## 1. Hearing for help
Once filled with the cheers of merriment, the stone brick walls of the Loud House sit in quiet candlelight, with [[Ruby Marsh]], buried in preparations, to keep the furniture company. Each step, light or heavy, cuts through the low murmurs and thoughtless noise. Ruby peers forward, turning towards everyone, and panning her eyes up and down before picking up she left off with a nod. However as everyone finally reaches the stairs, Donic's low voice calls behind them

>*"Next round'll cost ya double if you squeak that floorboard."*

Chuckling to himself, he explains that he's the Loud House's entertainer, lost daydreaming the past, but the party brought him back. 

>*Did that line about the floors get ya? A pity I'm missing out on your faces, must be priceless."*

Continuing the witty remarks, Donic mentions he wants help finding a [[Rebel Cache|cache]] of goodies his creator left behind. 

>*"If you're interested, come meet me by her old home. She lived towards the southwest, past the [[Abandoned Building]].*

## 2. Unlocking the entrance
Beneath the clarity of midday skies, the party spots Donic leaned against the cobblestone walls of an unassuming home. As their feet clamor against the roads, Donic spins his head and catches the party's trace, gesturing with a carefree wave that could pierce the heavens. With everyone back together, Donic detaches his hand, pushing it against the wall and feeling grooves in the texture. A few moments pass, and he finds a secret panel etched to fit his fingers which unlocks a passageway down into the tunnels. 

Reattaching his hand as he steps towards the rear of the party, Donic says
>*I ain't missing no chance to find that cache again. Lead the way, folks."

## 3. Tunnels Journey
An all too familiar aroma of stagnant air accompanies the dim lighting journeying down the tunnel. Pitter-patters of critters scurrying away through puddles are mostly muffled by the soil. Clearing his metaphorical throat, Donic starts making idle chatter

>"*Ya know, I've heard quite a tale about you lot. Saying you caused that 'spectacle' at [[The Festival of Granite Rain|The Festival]]. Folks love their gossip in this city, spreading it like fire, and all I need to do is soak it in. Quite the luxury, when ya think about it.*"

>*"Course, I know everybody spreading that rubbish is full of it. Only time I've ever stood in range of my own destruction was lookin' out from [[Rutmar Port|Rutmar]]. Ahem, point being, I know in my gut you didn't, but I can't prove it."*

If Donic is called on for [[The Trial]] he won't be much help other than vouching for integrity since he refuses to identify the one responsible. In the meantime, Donic knows your characters have rumors about themselves, so he's willing to trade the truth about yours for what he's heard around. Use this opportunity to nudge players towards other Hooks, letting them feel a sense of agency.

Examples:
- "*Regular comes in, ain't much for superstition, saying he remembers a ghost in his face and needed a pint to fix that*"
- "*Overheard Callie's husband talkin with Ruby, asking if he could hang a contract from her walls. She told [[Abraham Costa|Abe]] no, but knew she'd find people willing since it's him.*"

## 4. At the cache
Weaving their way around the last corner, a metal gate blocks the end of the corridor, illuminated by the powerful crackle of a single torch. Running hands and eyes alike across the tunnel walls, the party spots the [[Rotating Hand Puzzle]], an embedded locking system with 4 covers and a central ring. As Donic pats his hand against the stone, he asks someone to stand near the locks. Naturally, one of you obliges, and Donic remembers the riddle his creator told and recites it aloud

>*"Stare upon the life of hate our kin lead
>Feel the pain of those discarded by love
>Mourn the joys they were robbed of
>And scour the night sky for what our plights need"*

Taking his hand and removing it once more, he produces a written note version of the clue, passing it along to the nearest character.

## 5. Puzzle Solved
With 4 clicks the cache opens up, so the party returns Donic’s hand and steps inside. Plastered across the walls are forms of [[Rebels|rebellion]] iconography, even older prototype variations nobody has seen. In the corner, a foot high pile of coins glimmers, catching the party's eye. Neatly rolled into tubes and stacked on a shelf are dozens of failed schematics, accompanying the dust-ridden tinkering workbench, arcane table and alchemical equipment. Though Donic cannot fully see any of these, he tenderly taps and slides his hand across them.

>*"Ha, she really kept it here, all this time. This was the bench she had me sat on when I came to life, which means over there was where she made my heart."*

If anyone asks about the rebellion or Donic's connection to them, he'll go on about his creator. 

>*"Greatest damned 'rebel' there ever was, with brains the guard could only dream of. All she ever dreamed of was free peace for the world she loved. The rebels don't count me in their books, but given that Ruby trusts me to know about their tunnel, I think they flash a smile or two at me.*"

>*Help yourself to anything that ain't on this table. Just let me know when you wanna head topside."

Once everyone retraces their way back up to the surface, the sun shine behind Donic as he waves goodbye to the party. He'll remember the good they've done for an old machine like him. With that, the current scene fades to black, awaiting the next session's hook.

## Hiding Spots
The segment of the tunnels has a couple dead ends to it where the players can hide if they need to. Alternatively, a player could easily navigate their way back to the surface if the tunnels themselves are too cramped for them. All they would need to do is let the party know where they'd wanna meet back up with everyone else.
