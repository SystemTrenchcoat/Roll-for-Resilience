# Hook
___
*A rumor started spreading throughout the city that someone has picked up an ancient text that washed ashore. Nobody  seems to know who started the rumor or who currently possesses the notebook. Somewhere, somehow, the party now has possession of the notebook.*
# Story Beats
___
### 1. Discovery
___
The party is given the notebook.
	A torn notebook washes up in the port. It was picked up by [[Leonard Traco]] on his morning walk. A rumor started spreading throughout the city that someone has picked up an ancient text, and nobody has been able to understand what it is describing. One day, when one of the party members was on a stroll/shopping, Leonard calls them over and shows them the torn notebook.

### 2. The search for the notebook
___
#### Rebels:
Someone in a dark coat whispers to a party member one day “I know you have the book. We can shelter you from the guards if you had it over ” The shadowy figure then disappears. 
#### The High Trident: 
There are more priests walking around the city than normal. A note is slid underneath the party’s door at the inn they are staying at OR a person in a white robe tracks down the party and hands them the note.
> “Relinquish sacred codex unto moon spear’s keeping, forsake any gaze that dares to stray.” 

An easy 
#### Guards: 
Guards who are patrolling the city now randomly select citizens and question them about the rumor. A guard captain/normal guard stops them and asks the players about the book. Charisma check to determine whether they can fool the guard.

### 3. Decipher the notebook
The book contains hidden puzzles which the players can solve to find the history of Nervios. Here is a list of options:
- An image or symbol appears on a page when the page is dipped in salt water. 
- Pages might be written in some unknown language
- There may be hidden spells inside pictures
- Moonlight may change the text on the page to something else
- How to resurrect corpses
- A general cookbook
- Gunther’s cookbook
- A fantasy novel (Further investigation may show it describes the history of Nervios /first settlers on the peninsula)
- A shipbuilder’s blueprint book
- Umorist Teachings
- Part of a book series 
- Poetry
- An encyclopedia 
- A ritual that can calm or awaken the Ocean God


### 4. Consequences of their actions:
___
#### Option A: Give the book to the Guards
The party hands the book to the guards at the nearest guard outpost. The guards take the notebook and hand it over to [[Spandam Collins]]. He investigates the book and learns the book's origin and contents.  Standard procedure for a religious text is to hand it over to the High Trident, so he forms a small team of guards to transport the book to the high trident. 

>[!info] Perception Check
 DM rolls a perception check for the guards to see if they believe the book is the real book instead of a fake. 
  
DM rolls a D20 to see if the book is stolen during the transportation process. If the roll is above an 8, it is stolen and the rebels now have possession of the book. 
Alternatively, the book may be stolen during the transportation process by the rebels or the guard captain may hand it over to a rich noble. If the book is stolen, the guards will ask the players to help find the notebook. 
#### Option B: Give the book to the The High Trident
Players have been scheduled to meet with all 3 members of the high trident in a secret room. On the night of their meeting, the Moon spear greets them at the entrance of the church, and guides them into a meeting room where the Sea and Sun spear are already seated. They thank the party for handing over the book, and they go their separate ways.
#### Option C: Give the book to the Rebels
The rebels offer you a safehouse to stay in to evade capture from both the guards and the high trident. The players are brought to a hidden workshop/hideout. The rebels reveal that this is a missing book they have been trying to find. The rebels may reveal information about the book that the players may or may not know already from investigation checks. They also allow the players access to any of their safehouses if they need to escape the guards/high trident. 
### Option D: Keep the book
Players need to pass multiple semi difficult deception checks to make the guards, high trident, and rebels. If they fail, they are now being actively pursued by that group. This can lead the players into a Trial Session.
