# Content

### Hook
>After travelling through the tunnels under the city of [[Nervios]], completing strange challenges and besting dangerous creatures. Now with the opening of the mysterious door, the party beholds a strange sight. As the bright crack snakes and grows between the door and the wall, the players are greeted with the sound of many hushed voices, the clinking of many coins exchanging hands, and the shifting and rattling of bottles and boxes being passed about. Bioluminescent plants light up the market from pots hanging from ceilings or nearby gardens, illuminating the various ram shackled stalls and cloaked, masked figures milling about the market. They all speak in whispered, but lax tones. An oddity you’re not quite familiar with.

## Story Beats
---
This missions takes place after [[The Collapse of Central Stage]] scene, where the party needs to familiarize themselves with the various factions that hold sway over [[The Burrow]]. The party needs to complete a series of errands in order to convince Bethia to help them get back on the surface, since she needs to be sure that they are trust worthy enough to keep the secret of the Burrow from the guards.

### 1. The Mask Seller
Introductions to [[The Burrow]]
	As the players enter, they see many hanging bioluminescent plants casting a soft glow on the various dubious looking stalls and people in various different kinds of masks milling around them. A small tent set up in a nearby corner catches their eye as its owner waves them over. The owner doesn’t wear a mask, but instead they wear a veil of beads.
	As the players approach the tent, the mask seller inquires about their day and what brings them to [[The Burrow]]. If they said that they fell down here and need a way back up, they will inform them that [[Bethia Tallowwind]] might be able to help them with getting back to the surface. They also give them a little bits and pieces about [[The Burrow]], saying that it’s been here for quite some time, and that they’ve been here since it first came to be. They say that this space is a nice space for people to find things not normally found in [[Nervios]] proper, while also allowing others to breath away from [[The Nervios Eye]]. The Masks the players can get can then be personalized, which can be a nice way for the players to further personalize and define their characters (maybe a good option for the therapist to ask about the decisions behind them). If the players ask about getting back to the surface, the mask seller say their best bet is to talk to [[Bethia Tallowwind]], the leader of the smugglers about getting back up to the surface, especially if they want less attention from the guards.

As the party walks through the Burrow, they notice the denizens of the Burrow, while being masked to hide their identity, they talk in whispers that are lax and unconcerned with saying the wrong thing.


### 2. Bethia and the Request
Getting Passage
	As the players approach Bethia's incredibly large stand, they see [[Bethia Tallowwind]] quickly and smoothly slide over to the party, followed by [[Ms. Gotsome]] stoically a few steps behind, silently standing and judging the party. Bethia will greet players warmly, saying it's always nice to see new masks around the burrow (unless they didn't get any masks, then she simply smirks with her voice and says it is a bold choice, though not a dangerous one. No one here will snitch, though if they are ever discovered by the guards, it's a bit of extra security for those who can escape not having their face known, and those who can't not giving up their fellows by having the faces scryed through the gauntlets). She list off a couple items from a wide range to show off what they have on sale. If the players ask for a way to the surface and explain how they got there, Bethia offers to get the people back up to the surface discretely so that the guards aren't suspicious of them (They'll utilize one or two corrupt guards who are in charge of sorting through the rubble to say they found the survivors and that they weren't involved with the collapse of the stage). But in exchange, they need to do her a favor and help collect a special package from the [[Rebels]] that she warns the party not to peak inside the package so that the quality of the package isn't ruined.

The request is a quest that takes the party through multiple concerning parts, doing favors for each till they finally can start completing tasks and finish the quest.
	**Retrieve Bethia's Delivery**
	This quest is a small series of talks with the various parties around the Burrow, each one requiring a different item promised but delayed by another one of the parties. The chain begins and end with the rebels, going as follows:
	- From Bethia to the Rebels to retrieve her package
	- From the Rebels to the black smith Gregory Marsh to retrieve their order of swords
	- From the black smith Gregory to the Sea Scribe Gweneth Falzy to retrieve an blessing
	- From the Sea Scribe Gweneth to the food stall of Gunther Stone to retrieve her lunch
	- Help Gunther deal with the lunch rush at his stall to retrieve the lunch for Gweneth and start completing the chain of favors.

# The Rebels: 
When the players approach the rebels to get the object that Bathia asked for, they find Sheik lounging on a box, not really paying attention, Eleni looking over some documents and Tassos standing behind her, acting all stoic. When the party asks for the package, Eleni explains that while they have it, they can’t give it till they’ve been given their agreed upon compensation, with Sheik casually saying they don’t work for free. The party is directed to Gregory to retrieve the weapons that Bethia had promised in exchange for the box. 

If the players ask about the rebellion, Eleni and Tassos talk about how the rebellion seeks to free Nervios from the tyrannical rule of the guards and reopen the gates so that the people of Nervios no longer have to grovel at the behest of the nobles for food and supplies. Sheik simply states that they’re in it for the money, as there’s not much left for people like him to turn to.

# Gregory: 
When the players approach Gregory, they see that he is busy doing maintenance on a sword, one of the ones that the rebels ordered. He says that he’s almost finished with the order, he just needs a rune from the sea scribes to properly finish blessing it. Gregoy talks in a deep voice, carefully measured works. If the player ask why he’s down here, he answers that besides the obvious economic opportunity for being the main supplier for a rebellion, he says that under the current regime, he says that most of his orders are from the guard, ordering more mannicales, gauntlets, and bars for cells. He wants to make nails, steel bands for the outside of wheels, things that support and build the community instead of bringing fear and destruction. He wants his wife to come home from the bar telling stories from travellers and merchants of the great sights they see, of the amazing events and lives their neighbors are living, not the boisterous “War stories” of the guards harassing people and their idiotic public discussion of sensitive plans. And if that means making weapons so that the guards are overthrown and those dreams can be realized.

# Gweneth: 
The players met an old cranky lady pouring over old maps and blueprints. She’s the chief priest of Umorism in the city, and when asked about the rune she says that she’s not finishing it till she gets her lunch. It should have been here over an hour ago, and she can’t concentrate on magic on an empty stomach. If the players ask about Umorism, she explains that it is the old religion of Nervios, before the Church of Aquerism broke away from it to side with the guards and help to outlaw Umorism. While Aquerism teaches that the people must fear and worship the sea to survive its wrath, the Sea Scribes of Umorism taught the people that the sea was the ultimate challenger, the one that pushes humanity forward to new heights and possibilities. The sea shouldn’t be feared, but respected, and its challenge embraced thoughtfully. Going out in a storm is foolish and the sea has no respect for a fool.

# 3. Gunther's Food Pit
Gunther hasn’t been able to get Gweneth her food because his stall is so jam packed. He says that if the players help him with his orders, he’ll be able to finish the delivery orders. (He’ll also pay them some gold). The players need to use their skills creatively to help fulfill orders and deal with hazards. There is an event health pool that players contribute to by completing orders, while they also need to keep the event patience from dropping to 0. 

Hazards to patience: Not all things run smoothly in the kitchen, so each round a new hazard will appear that will present a new challenge for the players to react to and defeat. Random table to determine what hazard occurs, GM can either choose or roll. Don’t do duplicates, instead choosing/rerolling a new option. The players also win if there are no new options to pick for the hazard this round, having kept the crowd placated enough that Gunther was able to finish all the orders in a timely manner.

Time limit on each player's turn to decide what they do for their turn. A minute at first.

Patience pool (player health): 10.  

Enjoyment (event quota to meet): 0 out of 5 x (the amount of players in the party)

Each time the players do an action to serve the crowd, if they succeed the check, they raise enjoyment by 1. Difficulty 15.
