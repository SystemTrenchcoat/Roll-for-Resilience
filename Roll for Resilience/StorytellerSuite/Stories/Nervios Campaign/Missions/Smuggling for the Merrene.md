# Hook
___
A rumor is spreading through the city that the Merrene, the city’s wealthy family boogeyman, posted a contract for work that requires a team of [party size] people, wanting them to meet down by the docks.
The party stumbles near the job board as a new contract is being added to the board, with a well dressed man in goggles thanking the worker before turning to walk away.
Walking near the docks, they spot Edral standing alone holding an ornate pocket watch as everyone around them clears each passing minute. 
If the party has met Iniza before this mission, she tells them that a client was looking for a group of people to help shift around some less than legal wares, go ask for ‘Mister Dakhan’ down by the docks.
# Story Beats
___
### 1. Finding the representative
The party arrives at the dock specified in the contract, and finds Edral after making their way through the end-of-day market crowd. If the party found the quest through the job board, Edral recognizes them upon arrival.

### 2. Small Riddle
Edral presents the party with a small riddle, to get a read on their capabilities before giving them any of the information. He explains that although he will be disappointed, failing to solve the riddle in the allotted guesses will not count against them.
### **Conversational Riddles**
Riddles have allowed for those sharing goals to sift through the uninitiated for as long as intelligent life has existed, and these are no different. 

#### **Riddle 1:**
 A simpler riddle with no real consequence nor reward other than admiration and respect from Edral, potentially good in its own right. Edral asks for the answer in three guesses to at least one of the following:

|   |   |
|---|---|
|“I cannot speak, yet I always reply when spoken to”|Echo|
|“I am easy to life, but hard to throw”|Feather|
|“I can be broken without being touched”|Promise|




### 3. Details Unfold
Edral lays out the details of what the party is to assist in smuggling, they are to meet a Tiefling named Iniza in a tunnel beneath the city who will ask them another riddle. He passes a metal disc, a clue to Iniza’s riddle etched with the Merrene family coat of arms, and walks off.
#### Riddle 2:
Iniza’s riddle that Edral attempted to guide the player in getting the answer to with the disc. Players get one attempt, and upon failure must roll to save against gaining Stress and keep their composure. If the players fail, the disc is meant to serve as a backup to calm and regain the trust of Iniza. Iniza asks the player for the answer to:

- “I touch no thread, yet I make the finest pockets in the land. What am I?” The banks. Accept ‘coin’ or ‘gold’ as close enough
#### Disc Puzzle
Coin Description: A polished golden disk that glimmers in light’s rays. Proudly etched into the metal is the Merrene family coat of arms, on the side opposite the party’s clue. Larger than your average coin, this luxury novelty covers the entire palm of an average human.

In order to complete the job, Edral provides the party with a special metal coin. On one side, a short clue has also been put onto this coin, “Property of wealth’s giver.” This is meant to prime the players to be thinking of who gives the wealth to the people, with property serving a double meaning. Property as in belonging to, and property as in physical building.
### 4. Tunnels Entrance
Near the entrance to the tunnel, a nameless thug stands on lookout so the party can’t miss the entrance, and asks if the newbloods picked up on what the disc means. He lets the party know that established tunnel entrances generally have a puzzle lock, pulling back a panel to reveal the four button system on this door. He punches in the combination, and the party descends downwards.

### 5. Into the tunnels
Down in the tunnels, the party finds a hooded Iniza, hovering her hand near her waist as she asks the party her riddle. Whether through a proper answer, presenting the disc, or the party already having met her, the tension of the moment is dropped and Iniza walks the party towards where she’s keeping her cargo.

### 6. Moving the cargo
Iniza needs the party’s help moving all of her cargo onto the boats of her associates, making as quick and careful work of it as possible. 

#### Moving Cargo
While helping Iniza quickly and safely move all of the cargo onto the boats belonging to her and her associates, the party will need to make a series of successes, which they can help each other with to reduce risk. 

There is a total of two times the party size of crates, each requires at least one person to carry. Crates are a medium difficulty check, failing twice consecutively or crit failing to carry the crate will result in a broken crate. If two players carry a crate together, breaking a crate is changed to 3 consecutive fails. 

Iniza will be satisfied as long as half of the crates safely make it onto the boats, as she accounted for dealing with amateurs on this particular haul. Every crate over half rewards an extra 3-5 SCL on top of the 15 SCL base reward from Edral. On a complete success, Iniza says to tell Edral “Alas plenas” (AH-LAHS PLAY-NAHS)

When the party is finished, Iniza provides the party with where to meet back with Edral to tell him they did their job. As a parting gift, she rhetorically asks the party a third riddle, before giving them the answer as she leaves. “What can be touched, but can’t be seen? A heart”
#### Riddle 3:
Although this rhetorical riddle is dropped by Iniza as she departs, this is not meaningless chatter on her part, her wisdom here is meant to serve two parts. One, it's meant to clue the party in that there’s more to her than simply being a smuggler, she’s a girl with ideals. Two, this reminds the party to be thinking about redemption and change, even if for a brief moment.

### 7. Collect the reward
Having performed their part of the job, the party reports back to Edral to receive their reward for the contract. The party receives their reward by saying either the amount of crates out of the total or the specific code phrase from Iniza for a complete success. Any attempt to lie to Edral will immediately fail, do not allow checks for this, use this as an opportunity to reveal Edral as an Aasimar to the party by removing his goggles.

## Scaffolding
---
Considering the potential for this to be early into the party’s campaign, this mission provides incentive and reason for the party to stay together. Mechanically, this mission starts off simple with a low stakes riddle to allow the party to understand how they function in this instance. Stakes are increased when they are given a clue to the next riddle prior to hearing it, and can only make one guess. To compensate for this, two answers of a similar nature are explicitly allowed with the potential for more up to your discretion as the GM. Players should be able to feel and understand why these rules change, so that they still make sense and feel familiar. This mission works to scaffold potentially later missions where the party has less time or general room for error with solving riddles

## Hiding Spots
Once they are down in the tunnels, any of the players can either return to the surface or take a breather in one of the corners of the tunnel and hopefully become comfortable again. Any cargo that the players need to move can easily be accomplished by less characters, it simply increases the time needed depending on how many characters are hiding

## Motivators
After spending a few minutes without giving an answer to Edral, he will insist once again to wager any form of guess since there’s no consequence. If the party continues to not make any guesses, Edral will give them a hint to the first letter of the word.

Although Iniza knows that she has the nobles on her side, she still wants to avoid any unnecessary trouble from the guards, so she’s not going to let the party stand and mull over anything for too long. If the party is taking too long to answer her riddle, she will pull out a dagger and put up an imposing front to the party asking them who they are.
