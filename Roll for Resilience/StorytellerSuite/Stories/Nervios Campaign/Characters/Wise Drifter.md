## Description
Someone deliberately left a stranger to the party, acting similar to a spirit due to lacking both footsteps and pulse of life.

### Motivations
Apotheosis. For over seventy years Votiel has dove head first into research, searching for a way to better its craft and reach what it sees as the pinnacle of magic - transcending the material world. Now, it's finally reached that peak, and it wishes to demonstrate its perfection of knowledge by creating a portal. This is where the party comes in, Votiel has been watching the crowds of the city, and they caught its eye first at [[The Festival of Granite Rain]], quietly following shortly behind their chronicles.

### Secrets
The Wise Drifter withholds its true name, Votiel (VOH-TEE-EL), from everyone who encounters it on account of its awareness to the Fey's existence.

### Physical Appearance
Draped in the mystery of a copper cloak, the majority of the Drifter is obscured from sight apart from the light blue of the spiral glasses worn beneath its rounded hood. Both the legs and hands of this creature are not easily visible, further lending to its eerie and spiritual presence as it floats above the ground.
