## Description
The owner of the Loud House tavern-- a social hub for [[guards]] and [[commonfolk]] alike. Ruby tends to get carried away and party with her customers.

### Physical Appearance
Ruby has broad shoulders and arms replete with muscles from years of heaving kegs around as though they were feathers while working in her tavern. Her namesake ruby-red hair seems to have a life of its own, sitting boisterously on top of her head. 

### Vocal Performance
Ruby's rambunctious nature shines through her voice, a hearty projection of her honest whole self. Voicing her naturally means carrying this energy through volume, picking up speed and slowing back down as needed to convey cheer and excitement. For her mid-range, powerful, feminine voice, a tiny bit of "southern charm" could work wonders in bringing this character to life.

### Sample Dialog
- *Come in, come in. Sit on down, welcome to the Loud House*
- *Ready for the next round, folks? Try and keep up~*
- *Hey now, mind your manners or I'll toss you out myself*
- *I'm happily taken, honey. You'll have to find another sweetheart*

## Backstory
Ruby is a mother of two and the beloved wife to Gregory Marsh. She lives a simple life as a bartender and wants everyone to get along.
