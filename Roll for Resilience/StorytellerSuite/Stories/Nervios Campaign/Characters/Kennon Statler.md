# Physical Description
___
An older human man with a thick grey unibrow and horseshoe haircut. He has a large cleft chin and permanent wrinkles from a life of scowling. He wears simple sky-blue robes with a thick yellow belt wrapped tightly around his waist.

# Backstory
___
Kennon has been an Anchor in [[The Courthouse]] for the majority of his life. A life of judging has left him rather dull and apathetic to those who stand trial before him. Statler would rather finish a Trial quickly with an improper conviction than go through all the necessary motions.
