# Physical Description
___
A physically-imposing human woman who wears her emotions on her face. Her luscious jet-black hair spills over fluttering sky-blue robes, stopping short just before it reaches the thick yellow leather band that wraps snug around her waist. 
# Backstory
___
Janice has been an Anchor of [[The Courthouse]] since she was a young adult. Unlike [[Kennon Statler]], she enjoys the drama of court and remains an active participant, even if its only through judgmental audio cues such as sighs or tongue-clicks. Despite enjoying the drama of court, she would rather die then sit through an "obviously" cut and dry case. Unfortunately for Janice, she is not the best judge of character and many of those cases are not so obvious.
