## Description

Original owner of the [[Loud House Tavern]], ran alongside his best friend [[Kymil Rokian]], who served as the Innkeep for [[The Fluffy Queen Inn|The Fluffy Queen]]. Passed on the tavern to the [[Ruby Marsh|Marsh]] family when he passed of natural causes, upsetting Kymil.
