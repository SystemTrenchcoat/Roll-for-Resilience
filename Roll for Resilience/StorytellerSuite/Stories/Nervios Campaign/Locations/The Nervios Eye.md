# Description
___
In the center of the bay of [[Nervios]], a large spire towers over the city below. The foundation of the spire is wide and tall, and sits upon a small island made of sharp rocks and boulders. During high-tide, the island completely submerges under the bay, and the bottom of the tower gets hammered by the crashing waves of the ocean. As your eye follow from the bottom of the tower to the top, a sense of gloom overtakes you. This structure is larger than anything you've seen before. It is larger then the grand cathedral, taller then the great walls that surround the city and gargantuan even compared to the guard towers that loom over the streets. A top the structure rests a massive ornate eye made of gold and silver. The eye is tangible and real, yet its presence creates a feeling of constant surveillance. The pupil of the eye is said to follow you, no matter where you go. Whether within the city walls, or out at sea, you feel as you are being watched.

## Rope Bridge
On the Western face at the base of the tower, two parallel ropes extend from either side of the main entrance straight into the briny water of Rutmar Port.
- Large door in the western wall of the port. 
	- Two ropes extend from either side into the water. The dual ropes come out of the water at the base of the spire and connect to either side of the main entrance
	- On the inner side of the door, a large wheel and crank connect to the two ropes. Two guards stand nearby guarding the crank
- Reeling in the ropes reveals a large rope-bridge that connects the Spire to the door. Two guards sit protecting the door.

## Final Floor
- Lighthouse Mechanics
	- Glass lens dome-roof covered by a copper dome shutter-door. There is a small crack in one of the doors, allowing a small amount of moonlight to pass in during certain times of the year. 
	- Lens concentrates the moonlight into a beam of white light that shoots downwards into an angled mirror
	- An angled mirror sits atop a hefty turntable. A magic rune is painted into the center of the mirror. When the beam of light hits the mirror and rune, it is amplified and grows much larger in size.
- Wire Eye
	- Intricate structure made of gold and silver wire that creates an image of an eye. 
	- Pupil is made of gold, the iris is an empty space, and the sclera is made of silver
	- The eye hangs from the roof by bolts, suspended over the steep drop into the bay. 
	- The ornate pattern and weaving of wire casts powerful illusionary magic. The magic makes the Spire appear significantly larger to those inside and out. It also drastically alters the physical appearance of the spire, replacing a bold white and black lighthouse with a brutalist-gothic tower made of dark blacks and greys.

# History
___
This large spire was the first tower of observation that the guards built when they began using them shortly after coming into power. It is said that powerful magics are housed within the tower that allows them to keep constant vigilance over the only exit of the city; the docks.
