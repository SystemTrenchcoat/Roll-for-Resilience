## Description
For the souls of Nervios, this resplendent, ethereal cavern without a ceiling is the afterlife that awaits them on their journey towards ascension. Many await their reincarnation within these crystalline walls, having faced the [[Trial of Resolute Soul]] and not reemerged as one of the [[Church of Aequorism#***Drowned Souls***|Drowned Souls]].  

## History
Since the dawn of Nervios, the bright planes of the Expanse has existed as a dwelling for the honest souls. Stories say [[Aequorin]] himself felt such pride in the children following the spirit of his word that he created the Expanse so they may cherish each other for eternity.
