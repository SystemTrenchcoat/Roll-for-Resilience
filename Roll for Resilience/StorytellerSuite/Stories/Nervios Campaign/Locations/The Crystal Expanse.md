## Description
For the souls of Nervios, this ethereal plane is the afterlife that awaits them on their journey to ascension. Wondrous patterns shift overhead, high above the resplendent trench made by the rock walls, each playing with the mind and tricking it to feel beneath the tide. Reflecting across the length of slate trench walls are a colorful collection of crystals, drenching their surroundings with calming energy and dampening destructive magic.

Many souls awaiting reincarnation tuck themselves into crystal-dense corners, having faced the [[Trial of Resolute Soul]] and not reemerged as one of the [[Church of Aequorism#***Drowned Souls***|Drowned]]. They huddle against those soon to be significant and once beloved in equal measure.

## History
Since the dawn of Nervios, the breadth of the Expanse has existed as a dwelling for honest souls. Stories say [[Aequorin]] felt such pride in children following the spirit of his word that he chiseled stars into the Expanse to grant an eternal home to cherish one another
