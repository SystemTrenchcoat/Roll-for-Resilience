## Description
Eerily trapped in the stasis of decay on the western end of [[Nervios]], the Abandoned Building has laid uninhabited for generations, as none dare to take up residence here again. Decades worth of dirt cake each shattered window upon its facade, obscuring the outside world. Its interior layout has largely remained untouched, the stone main room sits atop the creaky entrance stairs, connected with the bed chamber and combination kitchen-workshop.

When the [[Wise Drifter|Drifter]] chose to test its work here, it took advantage of the holes within the roof, expanding one in particular to install a lens to harness the moon's focused light. Apart from this it has left the ceiling untouched, including the silky adornment of cobwebs.

## History
A story circulates this was the site of a human led break-in turned for the worse. As the story goes, several residents cursed themselves and charged the intruders to induce nightmares in all they could. What truth could be gleaned was those involved refused to speak of that night, lest they be reminded of the torment beneath their eyelids.

What was less etched into history was that this place was once a townhouse for around 8 non-humans, such as elves, dwarves, and even a goliath at one point. Though living in close proximity meant they bickered frequently, stains on the table from jokes creating food projectiles were equally common. Only one of them wasn't at home that night, piercing the wake of deafening silence on their return.
