## Description
Eerily trapped in the stasis of decay on the western end of [[Nervios]], the Abandoned Building has laid uninhabited for generations, as none dare to take up residence here again. Decades worth of dirt cake each window upon its facade, blackened to the point most confuse them for painted, until they notice the ones shattered. Its interior layout has largely remained untouched by disrepair, the stone main room still sits atop the creaky entrance stairs, connected with the bed chamber and combination kitchen-workshop. Since the [[Wise Drifter|Drifter]] chose to test its work here, it has refused to dust any of the numerous cobwebs outside the strict path it requires, including the silky adornments touching the ceiling.

## History
A story circulates that the Abandoned Building was the site of a human led break-in which turned for the worse. As the story goes, several residents cursed themselves and charged the intruders to induce nightmares in all they could. What truth could be gleaned was those involved refused to speak of that night, lest they be reminded of the torments beneath their eyelids.

What was less etched into history was that this abandoned building was once a townhouse for around 8 non-humans, such as elves, dwarves, and even a goliath at one point. Though living in close proximity meant they bickered frequently, stains on the table from jokes creating food projectiles were equally common. Only one of them wasn't at home that night, piercing the wake of deafening silence.
