# Description
___
The courthouse is a well known and highly active part of [[Nervios]]. Trials occur near-daily and crowds of curious and nosy citizens flock to the viewing windows to get a peek of the proceedings inside. It is an old structure, built with tall walls of stone brick layered with elegant decorative stone arches that hint at a more architecturally innovative time. It's massive oak doors are thick and weathered, creaking loudly whenever they are heaved open or shut. Along the lower walls, large glass viewing windows are set between the stonework, with long wooden benches positioned before them so the public can observe court hearings from outside.

## Holding Cells
An underground jail that sits beneath the busy floors of the courtroom. Here, criminals are held in dark and cramped cells as they bide time waiting for their trial. Upon entering through the teleportation rune, criminal detainees are met with  a small chamber lined with a luxurious black carpet that leads up to an iron-barred gate. The guards lead the criminals through the gate, past a blue and gold clerk's desk, and through a second barred checkpoint into a short hallway. Three braziers line the center of the hall, casting long fluctuating shadows on the smooth cobblestone walls. Running parallel on either side of the hall, 8 thick iron doors remain sealed and bolted from the outside. 

Prisoners are thrown into the isolated cells and held for days as they await trial. There are no windows, no clocks, and no way to distinguish day from night. Time passes only through the arrival of two meager meals a day.

>[!tip] Magic Dampening
> The air within the holding cells feels sterile and heavy. An arcane check reveals that magic is extremely weak and unviable in the basement due to the effects of a magic-dampening spell.
### Cell Description
The holding cells are small and dark with enough room for only one prisoner. The sole light is the orange glow of flame that seeps beneath the large solid-iron door and through the seams of its closed food-slot. A thin straw bedroll sits unevenly atop the cracked stone floor and thick cobwebs cling to the edges where wall meets ceiling.
