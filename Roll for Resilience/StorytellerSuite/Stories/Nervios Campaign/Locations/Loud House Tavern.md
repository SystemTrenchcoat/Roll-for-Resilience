## Description
Beneath the grounds of [[The Fluffy Queen Inn]] lies the Loud House Tavern, a bustling, lucrative hub of activity for everyone to come enjoy themselves, so long as they behave. Owned by [[Ruby Marsh]], she employs a tightly knit team to help her keep this place running smoothly.  

Furnished by rows of olive-wood tables, matched by 4 to 5 stools, the primarily stone interior is neatly maintained. Bricks upon the stone walls in general have clean mortar with the telltale faded color from aging, although the same organization cannot be said of the stones placed within the floor. Directly across from the entrance stairwell is a corner stage, presumably made from the same hardwood as the tables, rounded by the steps along its sides. Scattered throughout the tavern are a few baskets of plain white chalk, used by the patrons to entertain themselves and the staff to write menu specials.

## Secrets
Unbeknownst to the general public, tucked away behind the bar is a secret entrance to the [[Nervios Tunnels|Tunnel Network]] beneath [[Nervios]]. Ruby and her staff are entrusted with this secret by the [[Rebels|Rebellion]] 

## History
Long before the walls rose, and [[The Nervios Eye|The Eye]] with them, Loud House and The Fluffy Queen used to be one complete establishment. However, when Signoir, the dwarf who ran the tavern, passed away, he entrusted the Marsh family with his business. [[Kymil Rokian|Kymil]], riddled with grief, couldn't understand his departed business partner's decision. Why would one of his greatest friends willingly let humans inherit his century and a half hard work? He never bothered to ask the Marsh family if they had the answer.
