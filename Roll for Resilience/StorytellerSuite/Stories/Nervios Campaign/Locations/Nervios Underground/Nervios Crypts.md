# Description
Carved beneath the [[Sanctuary of Aequorin]], this cavern serves as the hall of the dead for [[Nervios]] as a whole. Littering the center are fragments of an ancient leviathan's spinal column, said to have lived within the [[Sunderwin]] in a time before the city was founded.
# History
As the city grew in popularity, undisturbed land to entomb and honor the dead became scarcer. New sectors were established as years went by, as families requested to be buried together, separate from others. 

In current day, only the nobles and upper class family for the city are able to afford a burial site. With no other option, it's become commonplace for bodies to discarded into the shallows past the docks.

## Flooded Sector
Composed of a lighter, tan rock, this sector of the crypts is one of the oldest, with some of the ancestral [[Merrene Family]] enshrined inside. Despite housing their dead, the Merrene were not the ones responsible for its seclusion and ultimately poor construction.

One destructive altercation between grieving families in the entrance to this sector caused a rock wall to give way. What followed was only natural for an enclosed space chipped away by erosion, ocean waters poured into the tunnel, rendering it inaccessible.

Details were later agreed upon that a fireball ignited a nearby barrel filled with alcohol, which detonated after the shockwave had already dislodged stones and began the leak.  A rather pricey bribe kept the [[Guards]] from imprisoning those involved that escaped the catastrophe, but none could prevent this event from being etched into the city's history.
