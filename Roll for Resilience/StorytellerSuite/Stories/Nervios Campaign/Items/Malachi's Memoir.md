# Description
___
## Physical Appearance
A dark blue book made of stained leather and parchment. It's title, "Malachi's Memoir" is embroidered on the front cover and edge in gold thread. An earthy stench of dried leather fills your nose as you flip through the time-worn pages; each one containing a date and title at the top.

## Page Information
The book is written and signed by  "Malachi". 
- Early in the book, he speaks of the purpose of the lighthouse and it's history. His ancestors had manned the tower for a century, guiding ships safely to and from the choppy shores of the bay. Now he holds the mantle of lighthouse-keeper and lives there with his wife and daughter. 
- He writes of the struggles of the times. Two neighboring cities have been at war, and Nervios starves and suffers as a result. The population grows hungry and restless, and even he is worried about his family.
- One day, he heard tale of the City Guard sealing the front gates with thousands of pounds of loose rubble and stone. They claimed it was for our protection from the outside world. He saw through them, knowing this was for their personal gain.
- The Guards grew increasingly powerful and hostile over the next few months. The port was shut off from the outside, causing Nervios to rely solely on fishing and their own produced goods. The economy strengthened as an influx of fishing and production jobs were in demand, and the city stopped starving.
- One day, a raven brought a letter signed by the Guards. They demanded Malachi turn over the keys to the lighthouse or face punishment. He righteously refused, and locked himself and his family away in the tower. With the Guard's threat increasing by the day, he snuck his family out through a secret escape hatch in the center of the base of the tower. Despite their best efforts for Malachi to join them, he chose to stand his ground and die in his ancestral home. This was the last entry in the memoir.
