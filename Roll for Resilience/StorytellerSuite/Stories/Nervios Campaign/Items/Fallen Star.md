# Description
Not much is known for certain about the Fallen Stars, apart from two major facts. The first, Fallen Stars are exceedingly rare and difficult to obtain, with the second being they exude an enormous signature of arcane energy.


## Physical Appearance
Fallen Stars are defined by their five rounded points protruding from a rock core. Known to emit a myriad of colorful bioluminescence, the star's featherweight core fits across the palm of most bipeds.

## Rules
From one generation to another, before finally being written, folklore managed to piece together the rules governing a Fallen Star

- You must speak your wish aloud
- Your wish must be plausible to your current environment
- You cannot influence death nor the dead
- You cannot interrupt the flow of time or aging

# History
Since the very first alleged Fallen Star was brought into civilization, mystical folklore has shrouded the truth of their capabilities. Some tout them dissolving into dust beneath harsh sunlight. Others claim they must be found and carried without uttering a word, lest it grant a wish you didn't mean.
