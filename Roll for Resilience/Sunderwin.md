## Description
Caressing [[Nervios]] on all sides, the Sunderwin sea is the saltwater home to the fantastical variety of fish responsible for the city's economy. Just as the Sunderwin surrounds Nervios, the greater continent coils around the Sunderwin everywhere but the northwest. As it snakes through a canal in the continent, it loses each morsel of salt, until it reconnects with the open ocean.

## History
Despite being locked in by the land, the Sunderwin extends to incredible depths. Nestled within them lies the tribal-rooted aquatic settlement where [[Nysaphia]] was born, though her family and a few others had to move to the shallows later. 

Unfortunately for most, the outskirts of the shallows were commonly the dropping point for many Nerviosi dead that could not afford the luxury of a burial.
