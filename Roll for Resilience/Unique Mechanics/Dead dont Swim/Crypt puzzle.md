Sealing the passageway in the [[Nervios Crypts]] down into the [[Nervios Crypts#Flooded Sector|Flooded Sector]] is a simple rotational puzzle resembling a skull with two notched hexagonal eyes. Inside the left eye specifically is a series of three numerals on every other notch: II, IIII, VI. Independent rotations can only be achieved by the right eye, as rotating the left eye also rotates the right by two notches in the same direction. 

## Feedback
- Gears can heard winding and whirring behind the wall on a partial solution

> [!faq] Clues
> - (Easy passive investigation) Immediate signs of wear leave one part of the answer rather clear, with one notch scratched further down towards the center of the right eye. 
> - Etched on a nearby stone: *“Not a single more than those left seen”*



>[!success] Solution
Align the scratch on the right with the top of the skull, and with six on top of the left.
