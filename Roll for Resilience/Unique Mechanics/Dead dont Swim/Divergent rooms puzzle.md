Testing the cooperative efforts of the party, this puzzle consists of a main room with two adjacent chambers. In order to lower the gates into the adjacent rooms, a central wheel in the main area must be turning. 

To open the gate forward, two switches hidden behind simpler puzzles must be activated.

## Larger puzzle Feedback
- Main chamber gets brighter when switches are pulled as light pours in
- Gate forward rattles when the first switch is pulled, but doesn't slide up

## Puzzle A
On the wall sits two concentric rings, which spin in opposite directions when a color corresponding lever is pulled. A ring is composed of five segments printed with roman numerals (I, II, III, IIII, V), and must follow these rules:

- Can only spin in 1 direction
- Must stop spinning before it can continue
- Lever or Horn needs to be recentered after each pull

### Feedback A
- Both rings have an indicator to show which position is currently active.
- Whirring gears can be heard inside the walls while a segment is correctly placed, outer gears come from the left and inner gears come from the right.
- When a ring stops spinning, it produces a clicking sound

>[!faq] Clues for A
>- When a segment finishes spinning into the correct segment, it clicks twice.
>- A discarded stone tablet nearby reads "Your switch lies inside half the clock's exterior, a purposeful print."

>[!success] Solution: A
>Position the outer ring to IIII and the inner ring to II
## Puzzle B
Numerous cubes of varying weights litter one end of an other wise empty room. Towards the other end lies a golden painted balance scale whose pans are supported by the lime green tentacles of a creature with circular magenta eyes and a mischievous fanged grin.

Each of the aforementioned cubes has a weight assigned to them, as follows
- 4 blue cubes weighing 1 Unit
- 3 white cubes weighing 2 Units
- 2 grey cubes weighing 5 Units
- 1 black cube weighing 10 Units

If a character chooses, they can also sacrifice an item to use in the puzzle. Convert every 2 pounds of the item into 1 unit, and discard any leftovers. 

Characters investigating the cubes will discover the following
- (Crit fail) Could all these cubes really make a sphere? 
- (Failed) These are definitely cubes, maybe from the same set.
- (Easy ~DC5) Each of the colored cubes are a metallic and equally sized.
- (Medium ~DC10) A set of metallic cubes, with colors matching the small numbers engraved onto each of them.

## Feedback B
- Scales rebalance as items are placed
- Eyes on the scale open and close depending on their corresponding pan

> [!faq] Clues for B:
> - Succeeding an easy investigation on or near the scales shows the undersides of the pans engraved with symbols. The left is marked by a V and the right a II.
> - When the correct number of items are placed on a pan, the corresponding eye closes

>[!check] Solution: B
>Balance the scales with five items on the left and two on the right.
