## Description
Embedded into the tunnel walls outside the [[Rebel Cache]] is a central spinning wheel and four covers arranged above it. On approach, one of the (incorrect) covers is currently open to demonstrate that the key of [[Donic]]'s hand is meant to fit inside. 
## Feedback
- When a correct part of the puzzle is set into place, a growing series of clicks and shifting gears can be heard. Clicks are reset on incorrect entry
- When an incorrect part is activated, all of the covers close and a spout of ice magic sprays from the wall for a moment before resetting. Party members within 5ft of the puzzle must make a dexterity save or take 1d8 damage.

>[!faq] Clue
>Stare upon the life of hate our kin lead
Feel the pain of those discarded by love
Mourn the joys they were robbed of
And scour the night sky for what our plights need

>[!success] Solution
> Each line of the clue has a word tied to a symbol on the wheel, indicating where in the sequence it needs to be activated. Ordering the clues should give the players the following sequence: 
> 
> Eye, Hand, Tear, Crescent.
