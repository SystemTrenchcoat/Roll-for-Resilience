Typically this judgement is faced by dead souls within [[The Crystal Expanse]] in order to continue their reincarnation cycle, however instead our party must face it to escape [[Nervios]] with their lives in their own hands. Everyone is presented with the imagery of an unrecognizable, tall humanoid statue with red crystal fractals emerging from its chest, face, and hands.

Characters with a passive perception or investigation of 15, or who roll a 15, will notice that what seemed to be a statue is actually a petrified corpse, with the blood red of crystal shards piercing their stone skin and flowering from inside them. For 17 or higher, a character will also feel a very sudden spike of energy around the crystals themself, and then perform an easy to medium history check to see if they successfully recognize the energy for a more potent variant of the mind reading gauntlets.

While each character undergoes the crystal's trial, they must make a series of three to five mental based saves in order to contain impactful memories in their life to their body. For each check there are varying degrees of success or failure, controlling the intactness and clarity of auditory elements, and whether the party sees apparitions of others present in the memory. Exact consequences are as follows:

| Below “Easy” Checkpoint        | Apparitions manifest of the current memory, a clear recreation of the visual and auditory elements |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Between “Easy” & “Medium”      | Disembodied voices echo along the walls, with nearly intact sentences.                             |
| Between “Medium” & “Hard”      | Only key fragments of sentences slip through the cracks, spilling outwards from the character.     |
| Reaching “Hard” or going above | Nothing. Your companions are treated to deathly silence, as if sound itself were stolen from them. |

If no one wants to be the one to initiate the trial willingly, [[Aequorin|Malachi]] can force them to undergo it by means of his avatar. A player forced into the trial must take disadvantage or penalty on their first save.

Another player can choose to act as a conduit for someone’s trial, holding onto the corpse and transferring the crystal’s energy through their body. The player receiving the trial takes advantage or a bonus on the save, however the conduit receives intact versions of all memories regardless of roll.
